Design direction

Library as the academic search and save surface

This screen should feel more like a useful catalog than a feed. Search stays first, filters stay close, and the content is grouped by the things students actually remember: course, type, and recency.

Primary job Help a student find a course material fast, then save or open it with minimal friction.
Navigation state The Study bottom nav keeps Library active, separate from the marketplace bottom nav.
Filter model Expose quick filters inline and keep deeper filters in a compact sheet or drawer later.
